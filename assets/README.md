# Embedded assets

New art and sound from synthwave-pixel's Ultima III port
(https://github.com/synthwave-pixel/ultima3, `web/public`), by Leon McNeill,
under the MIT License in `LICENSE`. Only the port's own work is here:

- `graphics/<Set>-Exodus|Fountain|Rod|Shrine|TimeLord.png`: the title logo
  and the four full-window scenes, drawn per tile set.
- `graphics/Standard-Tiles.png`: the Standard set's new figures.
- `graphics/Apple II Color-Tiles.png`: the Apple II Color tiles rebuilt from
  the Apple II bitmaps.
- `graphics/<Set>-UI.png` for Standard, Apple II Color and the five PC sets:
  each set's own frame, cursor and moons.
- `sounds/*.wav`: the Standard chip-tune effects (tools/chip-sfx.ts).

LairWare's own sheets, scenes, dungeon art, sampled effects and music are
not embedded: they come from the player's import of the Macintosh release
(`data/mac`). The game's name, maps and music are Origin Systems'.
